Privacy Policy

Effective Date: May 23, 2026

Infinite Shadow Jump Ninja is a single-player offline arcade game. This policy explains how the game handles information when you play it.

Information We Collect

The game stores gameplay progress, level stars, coins, and high score records locally on your device. We do not require an account and we do not ask you to submit personal information inside the game.

Network and Third-Party Services

The game is designed for offline play. The app template may include advertising, analytics, security, or crash-related SDK components. If those services are enabled in a distributed build, they may process device, diagnostic, or advertising-related data according to their own policies and Apple platform rules.

How Information Is Used

  • To save your level progress and high score records.
  • To maintain app stability, security, and basic game functionality.
  • To support advertising display if ads are enabled in the release version.

Children's Privacy

Infinite Shadow Jump Ninja does not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe personal information has been provided to us, please contact us so we can review and address the issue.

Data Control

You can remove locally stored game progress by deleting the app from your device. Reinstalling the game may reset local progress unless your device or operating system restores app data.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy when the app changes. The latest version will be made available on this page.
